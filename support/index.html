Quick answers

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Open the Premium screen in IN-BETWEEN and use Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that originally purchased the subscription.

Can I use IN-BETWEEN without Calendar or Location access?

Yes. Calendar and Location permissions are optional. Certain contextual features may be more limited when those permissions are disabled.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@in-between.app and include what you were doing, what happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Screenshots are helpful when available.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Apple manages App Store subscriptions. You can manage or cancel an active subscription from your Apple ID subscription settings.
